Sustainability & Operational Risk Management

Addressing the critical balance between mineral extraction demands and long-term environmental protection.

Key Sustainability & Operational Bottlenecks

  • Tailings & Toxic Waste Management: Improper handling of mining washing sludge and waste rock risks contaminating local water tables and surrounding agricultural land.
  • Monsoon Vulnerability & Mudslides: Heavy rainfall in Maharashtra and MP mining belts triggers slope failures in open-cast pits, halting haulage roads entirely.
  • Equipment Opex & Diesel Leakage: Sub-optimal rock fragmentation from poor blasting density leads to secondary crushing requirements and excessive diesel burning (12–15% excess).
  • Workforce Safety in Deep Shafts: Manual ventilation monitoring in underground operations like Balaghat exposes workers to toxic gas pockets unless automated telemetry is enforced.
